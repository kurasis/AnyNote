Revoking a device also revokes its delegated descendants. Independent devices remain. Last contact is server metadata and cannot prove that a physical device is safe. Local drafts are retained.
    

    

    Recover on a fresh installation
    First restore account sign-in. Load your offline account recovery kit in this trusted client, then prepare a fresh installation with a strong local phrase. The kit is never uploaded. Recovery activates fresh keys and revokes all old devices and sessions; key maintenance may still be needed. A stolen kit requires a new identity and independent verification, not repeated resets.
    If all copies of the kit and keys are lost, personal content cannot be decrypted by an administrator.
